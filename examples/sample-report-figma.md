   - Evidence: Penpot: [Penpot MCP Server Beta Launch](https://community.penpot.app/t/push-the-penpot-mcp-to-its-limits-join-the-beta-test/10363); Sketch: [AI for Designers - Sketch MCP Server](https://www.sketch.com/ai/)

Competitors like UXPin are differentiating by emphasizing AI that works with actual design systems rather than generic output, so Figma should pivot from general 'AI' messaging to showcasing precision-focused workflows that emphasize human intent over automation.
   - Evidence: UXPin: [UXPin Forge Launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/uxpin); UXPin: [Launch of Forge AI Design Assistant](https://uxpin.com/forge)
